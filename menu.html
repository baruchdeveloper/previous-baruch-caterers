    FAQ

    Menu planning questions, answered simply.

    Can the menu be customized?

    Yes. Baruch Caterers can customize the menu based on your event type, guest count, cuisine preference, and budget.

    Do you provide only vegetarian food?

    We provide vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and mixed menu options for different event needs.

    Do you cater outside Chennai?

    Yes, depending on the event size, date, and location. Contact us for availability.

    Do you provide serving staff?

    Yes. We can arrange serving staff, buffet supervisors, and complete service support.

    Minimum guest count?

    The minimum guest count depends on the package and event location. We will guide you after understanding your requirement.

    How early should I book?

    For weddings and large functions, earlier booking is recommended so menus and service teams can be planned properly.
