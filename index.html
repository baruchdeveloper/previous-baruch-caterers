About Baruch Caterers

Traditional taste, modern presentation, professional service.

Founded by Emmanuel Gnanaseelan, Baruch Caterers began with a simple mission: to bring blessed flavors to Chennai's most cherished moments. Today, the Baruch name is trusted by families, churches, organizations, and businesses for quality, hygiene, and exceptional taste.

Every wedding, birthday celebration, church function, and corporate event we cater is treated as our own, with careful planning, premium buffet setups, and warm hospitality.
